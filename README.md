# Missed lesions in brain tumour MRI segmentation: scoping review data

Data, AI prompts, code and supplementary material for a scoping review (PRISMA-ScR) of how lesion-level misses are measured and how their risk is estimated in brain tumour MRI segmentation and detection. Search dates: 9–10 October 2026.

## Screening tables (`data/screening/`)

| File | Records | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `round1_screening.tsv` | 5190 | PubMed, OpenAlex, arXiv and Europe PMC records after de-duplication: first-round decision, re-checks, final decision |
| `wos_additional_screening.tsv` | 417 | Web of Science records not found in the sources above |
| `scopus_additional_screening.tsv` | 940 | Scopus records not found in the sources above |
| `citation_tracking_screening.tsv` | 406 | Records found by citation tracking |
| `random_sample_classification.tsv` | 100 | Random PubMed sample used to estimate how many studies report only global voxel-level metrics |

Decision labels: `Include` (taken to the full-text stage), `Exclude`, `Unclear`, `Reference` (lesion-level uncertainty work outside brain tumours, reported separately). Reason codes E1–E9 are defined in Table S3 of the Supplementary Methods; `F0` means the full text could not be retrieved; `E4 (to be verified)` marks records first excluded on the title alone and later re-screened with the abstract; `Other full-text reason` marks exclusions at the full-text stage for reasons outside the numbered codes. `line` is the review line (`Metric` or `Risk`; `Review` for reviews). `round1_*`, `recheck_*`, `abstract_rescreen_*`, `blind_review_*` and `blind_recheck_*` are the successive AI passes described in the Supplementary Methods; `final_*` is the decision used for the flow diagram. Tumour type and evaluation unit follow the definitions in Table S4.

The working files contain a free-text reason for each decision, written in Chinese. These notes are not published; the decisions and codes are. Titles in Chinese are replaced by `[non-English title omitted]`.

## Reproducing the statistics and figures

```
pip install matplotlib numpy pillow
python3 code/stats.py          # writes data/summary_statistics.md
python3 code/make_figures.py   # writes figures/*.pdf and *.png
```

Both scripts read `supplement/S5_extraction_table.tsv`. The numbers in the flow diagram (Fig. 1) are entered in `code/make_figures.py` and are described in the Supplementary Methods (section 1.7 and the PRISMA-ScR flow).

## Not included

- Database exports. Web of Science and Scopus records are licensed; PubMed, OpenAlex, arXiv and Europe PMC exports are not redistributed either. Every record is identified by DOI, PMID or arXiv number, and the search strings in the Supplementary Methods re-create the sets.
- Full-text PDFs and the abstracts shown to the human raters (publisher copyright).
- Individual labels of the two human raters; the agreement statistics are in the Supplementary Methods (Tables S1, S2).
- Intermediate output files of the AI runs and utility scripts (they contain Chinese working notes; results are incorporated in Tables S5 and S6).
- No patient data were used.
